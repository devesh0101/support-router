# Refund Policy

## Standard Refunds
Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days to the original payment method.
Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase.
After 30 days, only partial refunds or account credits are issued.

## Dispute Resolution
For payment disputes or chargebacks, escalate immediately to the billing team.
Document all communication with the customer before escalating.
Never promise a refund timeline you cannot guarantee.

## Exceptions
Annual plan refunds are prorated based on months remaining.
One-time setup fees are non-refundable.
