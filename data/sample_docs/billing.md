# Billing FAQ

## Duplicate Charges
If a customer reports being charged twice, verify the transaction history first.
Duplicate charges are typically resolved within 3-5 business days.
Issue a full refund for the duplicate transaction and send confirmation.

## Subscription Changes
Subscription upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Prorated credits are applied automatically when upgrading mid-cycle.

## Payment Failures
If a payment fails, the system retries after 3 days, then 7 days.
After two failed attempts, the account is suspended but data is retained for 30 days.
Send a payment failure notification email after each failed attempt.
